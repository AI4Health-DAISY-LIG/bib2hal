Set up bib2hal

A few details, once. They are saved on this computer only.

Who you are

Used to recognise you among the authors and to spot HAL records not yet linked to your IdHAL.

Your labs

The labs and institutions you have belonged to — current and past (PhD, previous positions). Nothing is filled in automatically: for each paper, you will click the ones that apply at the time of the work.

Your disciplines

Pick the HAL disciplines you publish in. For each paper the closest one is suggested, and you confirm it in Review.

HAL account

Your hal.science account, used to submit your publications. Your login is checked directly with HAL; the password is sent only to HAL and is never written to a file.

Import your publication list

A BibTeX file exported from Google Scholar, Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote or ORCID (how to get one ↗). Each reference is completed from Crossref and looked up in HAL. Nothing is sent to HAL at this step.
