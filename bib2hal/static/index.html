bib2hal

Who you are

Used to recognise you among the authors and to spot HAL records not yet linked to your IdHAL.

Your current affiliation

HAL structure ids of your current lab. They are pre-filled only for works published from the year below on; older works (PhD, previous positions) are left for you to fill in.

Your disciplines

Pick the HAL disciplines you publish in. For each paper the closest one is suggested, and you confirm it in Review.

HAL account

Your hal.science account, used to submit your publications. Your login is checked directly with HAL; the password is sent only to HAL and is never written to a file.

Import your publication list

A BibTeX file: Google Scholar (select all → Export → BibTeX), Zotero, ORCID, publishers… Each reference is completed from Crossref and looked up in HAL. Nothing is deposited at this step.
