Guide

How to use bib2hal

About 30 minutes for a first import of a few dozen papers. You will need: your publication list as a .bib file (how to get it), and your HAL login.

Start the application

  1. Download the file for your system from the download page.
  2. Double-click it. A small window opens and the interface appears in your web browser. Keep the small window open while you work — closing it stops bib2hal.
    The first time, your system warns that the application is not signed: on Windows click More info → Run anyway; on macOS right-click the file → Open → Open. Details.
  3. Your settings and files are kept in Documents/bib2hal on your computer.

1 · Settings (once)

The progress bar at the top shows what is left to fill in. Everything is saved on your computer only.

  1. Your name as it appears in your references, written Family, Given (e.g. Muller, Sandrine), and your IdHAL (shown in your HAL account, e.g. sandrine-muller). bib2hal uses them to recognise you among the authors and to spot HAL records not yet linked to you.
  2. Your labs: search each lab you have belonged to (current and past) by name or acronym and click the right result (prefer the ones marked VALID). Nothing is filled in automatically: in Review you will click, for each paper, the lab(s) you belonged to at the time.
  3. Your disciplines: search HAL's list (in French or English) and add the ones you publish in. For each paper, the closest one will be suggested.
  4. Sign in to HAL with your hal.science login. bib2hal checks it with HAL; tick Remember me to stay signed in on this computer (stored in your system keychain, never in a file).

The set-up checklist. "Save and continue to Import" becomes available once name, lab and disciplines are set.

2 · Import

  1. Choose your .bib file and click Analyse.
  2. bib2hal completes each reference from Crossref and looks it up in HAL — about one second per reference, to stay polite with HAL's servers. Nothing is sent to HAL at this step.
  3. At the end you see how many references are to deposit, already in HAL (skip), in HAL but not linked to you (attach), duplicates, or to check.

3 · Review

The list on the left shows all your references; click To validate to see only what needs your attention. Click a reference to see it on the right.

ActionMeaningWhat to do
depositNot in HAL.Check type, disciplines and your affiliation, then Validate & next.
checkNeeds your decision.Read the amber box: it says why (homonym, unknown type, no Crossref match…). Fields to verify are outlined in amber. Then It's fine → deposit or Not mine → skip.
attachIn HAL, not linked to your IdHAL.Open the HAL link and add it to your publications in HAL (Mon espace → Mes publications).
skip / duplicateNothing to do.—

Red outline = required before deposit. Amber outline = please verify.

Tips

  • Several at once: tick references on the left (shift-click for a range, or "select all shown"), then set an action, a type or a discipline for all of them.
  • Affiliation at the time: click the lab(s) offered under "Your affiliation(s) for this paper", or search another one there.
  • Full text: to attach a PDF you are allowed to share (usually your accepted manuscript), click Attach a PDF….
  • Only the fields HAL needs are shown for each type: a thesis asks for the institution and supervisor(s), a conference paper for the conference city, country and date…
  • Come back later: your review is saved as you go. To add papers, update your .bib and choose Analyse this file in Import: what you already reviewed is kept, only new references are analysed. Export as .bib gives you back your list with the DOIs found and your corrections.
  • Preview TEI shows exactly what will be sent to HAL.

4 · Submit

If HAL refuses some records, only those need another look: they appear under the HAL errors filter in Review, with an explanation of what to fix (for example, a consortium listed as an author, or a thesis without its supervisor).

  1. Click Check with HAL: HAL validates each selected record completely and creates nothing. Each line then shows "check passed" or HAL's error message.
  2. Click Submit to HAL… and confirm. Each record is sent under your account; a reference already submitted is never sent twice.
  3. HAL moderators review each record before it becomes public. Follow them in your HAL account (Mon espace → Mes dépôts).

Keep it up to date

Deposit new papers in HAL first, at acceptance (HAL then sends them to ORCID, and Google Scholar indexes HAL). Once or twice a year, export a fresh list and import it again in bib2hal to catch what slipped through.

Questions

Is my previous review lost if I import again?

No. The previous review is kept as a dated backup in your bib2hal folder.

bib2hal says HAL answered with a protection page.

HAL's servers are protected against robots. bib2hal stops immediately rather than insisting. Wait a few hours; if it persists, contact HAL support.

Can I use it without the interface?

Yes, everything is available from the command line: see the README.
